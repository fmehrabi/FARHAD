       PROJECT 1
       GENERATIVE AI
  ========================== -->

  <article class="research-project">

    <h4 class="research-project-title">
      Managing Generative AI–Enabled Innovation: Evaluation Capacity, Organizational Learning, and Implementation Dynamics
    </h4>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      Manuscript Under Review · Technological Forecasting and Social Change
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-summary">
      This study examines how organizations can manage generative AI-enabled
      innovation by developing evaluation capacity, strengthening
      organizational learning, and managing the dynamics of implementation.
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      <strong>Keywords:</strong>
      Generative AI · AI-Enabled Innovation · Organizational Learning ·
      Evaluation Capacity · Organizational Decision-Making
    </p>

    <details class="expandable-details">

      <summary class="details-toggle">
        View details
      </summary>

      <div class="details-content">

        <ul class="research-project-details">

          <li>
            Developed a theory-driven System Dynamics model to examine
            the relationships between AI-generated candidate ideas,
            human evaluation capacity, organizational knowledge,
            and the active innovation portfolio.
          </li>

          <li>
            Evaluated alternative organizational interventions through
            policy simulation, global sensitivity analysis,
            and robustness assessment.
          </li>

          <li>
            Applied interpretable machine learning, including Linear
            Regression and Regression Trees, to identify conditions
            associated with policy performance.
          </li>

          <li>
            Used Python and Scikit-learn for data preparation,
            model implementation, cross-validation, and evaluation.
          </li>

        </ul>

      </div>

    </details>

  </article>


  <!-- =========================
       PROJECT 2
       AGENT-INSPIRED ADAPTIVE ALLOCATION
  ========================== -->

  <article class="research-project">

    <h4 class="research-project-title">
      When Does Agent-Inspired Adaptive Capacity Allocation Improve Circular Resource Productivity?
    </h4>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      Manuscript Under Review · International Journal of Productivity and Performance Management
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-summary">
      This study investigates when adaptive capacity allocation improves
      circular resource productivity under competing maintenance and
      recovery demands, with particular attention to uncertainty
      and operational trade-offs.
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      <strong>Keywords:</strong>
      Agent-Inspired Adaptive Decision-Making · Circular Economy ·
      Resource Productivity · Capacity Allocation · Operational Strategy
    </p>

    <details class="expandable-details">

      <summary class="details-toggle">
        View details
      </summary>

      <div class="details-content">

        <ul class="research-project-details">

          <li>
            Developed an agent-inspired adaptive decision model to
            investigate alternative capacity allocation policies
            in circular production and recovery operations.
          </li>

          <li>
            Integrated System Dynamics with Robust Decision Making,
            Scenario Discovery, and sensitivity analysis to evaluate
            policy performance under uncertainty.
          </li>

          <li>
            Applied Latin Hypercube Sampling, Partial Rank Correlation
            Coefficients, CART-based Scenario Discovery, and
            Random Forest Regression.
          </li>

          <li>
            Implemented the analytical and machine learning procedures
            using Python and Scikit-learn.
          </li>

        </ul>

      </div>

    </details>

  </article>


  <!-- =========================
       PROJECT 3
       DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION
  ========================== -->

  <article class="research-project">

    <h4 class="research-project-title">
      An Analysis of Value Creation in the Digital Transformation Process of E-commerce using a System Dynamics Approach
    </h4>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      Manuscript Under Review · Journal of Information Technology Management
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-summary">
      This research investigates how digital transformation factors
      influence value creation in an e-commerce context, examining
      the relationships between organizational capabilities,
      ecosystem conditions, and digital transformation outcomes.
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      <strong>Keywords:</strong>
      Digital Transformation · E-commerce · Value Creation ·
      Organizational Capabilities · Digital Strategy
    </p>

    <details class="expandable-details">

      <summary class="details-toggle">
        View details
      </summary>

      <div class="details-content">

        <ul class="research-project-details">

          <li>
            Developed a System Dynamics model informed by operational
            reports and expert consultation to examine the effects
            of digital transformation factors on value creation.
          </li>

          <li>
            Developed causal loop and stock-and-flow structures to
            represent the interrelationships among key organizational
            and digital transformation factors.
          </li>

          <li>
            Examined five key drivers: Government Guidance,
            Collaboration, Entrepreneurship, Customer Demand,
            and Data Privacy.
          </li>

          <li>
            Conducted simulation-based scenario analysis,
            model assessment, and sensitivity analysis to explore
            the implications of alternative transformation conditions.
          </li>

        </ul>

      </div>

    </details>

  </article>

  ========================== -->

  <article class="research-project">

    <h4 class="research-project-title">
      Strategic Analysis of Multi-Sided Platforms: A Case Study of Snapp
    </h4>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      Course Project · Advanced Strategic Management · 2022
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-summary">
      This project analyzed the strategic evolution of Snapp as a
      multi-sided digital platform and examined its expansion
      from ride-hailing into adjacent markets.
    </p>

    <p class="research-project-meta">
      <strong>Keywords:</strong>
      Digital Platforms · Ecosystem Strategy · Platform Envelopment ·
      Network Effects · Multi-Sided Markets
    </p>

    <details class="expandable-details">

      <summary class="details-toggle">
        View details
      </summary>

      <div class="details-content">

        <ul class="research-project-details">

          <li>
            Applied the Platform Envelopment framework to analyze
            how Snapp leveraged its existing ride-hailing user base
            to expand into food delivery, fintech, and retail markets.
          </li>

          <li>
            Examined ecosystem dynamics through cross-side network
            effects and multi-homing risks among supply-side users
            to assess platform retention challenges.
          </li>

          <li>
            Developed strategic recommendations for transitioning
            from rapid growth toward sustainable profitability,
            including mechanisms for increasing switching costs
            and strengthening platform retention.
          </li>

        </ul>

      </div>

    </details>

  </article>
